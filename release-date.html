When does GTA 6 come out?

Updated October 20, 2026

GTA 6 comes out on Thursday, November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar confirmed the date with the launch trailer on October 15, 2026. Digital preloads open on November 12.

Delay history

  • December 2023First trailer released, with a 2025 release window.
  • May 2025Release moved to May 26, 2026.
  • November 2025Release moved again to November 19, 2026.
  • October 15, 2026Launch trailer confirms November 19, 2026.

What time will GTA 6 unlock?

Rockstar has not posted unlock times yet. Big console launches often unlock at midnight local time for digital copies. We will update this page when official times are announced.

Is there a PC release date?

No. Rockstar has only announced console versions. Some reports suggest a PC version in early 2027, but nothing is confirmed.
