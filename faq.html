GTA 6 FAQ

Updated October 20, 2026

When is GTA 6 coming out?

November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. See the release date page.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not at launch. No PC date has been announced.

Is GTA 6 on PS4 or Xbox One?

No. It is only announced for current-generation consoles.

Will GTA 6 have online multiplayer at launch?

Reports say online will not be available on day one. Rockstar has not shared an online release date.

Where is GTA 6 set?

In the fictional state of Leonida, which is based on Florida and includes Vice City.

Who are the main characters?

Lucia and Jason, a criminal duo.

When can I preload GTA 6?

Digital preloads open November 12, 2026.
