GTA 6 file size: how much space do you need?

Updated October 21, 2026

Rockstar has not announced the official file size yet. Most estimates put GTA 6 at around 150 to 200 GB, and a day-one patch could add more. To be safe, plan on clearing about 250 GB before preloads open on November 12, 2026.

What we know

Official size
Not announced. It should show on the PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store listings once preloads open.
Estimates
About 150 to 200 GB for the base game, possibly 200 to 250 GB with the day-one patch.
Preload date
November 12, 2026 (digital copies)
Physical copies
Expect a large install and a download even with a disc or code in a box.

Is the 676 GB Xbox screenshot real?

No. A screenshot showing GTA 6 at about 677 GB spread on social media, but several sites have called it fake. Neither Rockstar nor Microsoft has posted a size.

Will it fit on my console?

A PS5 or Xbox Series X has roughly 667 GB of usable space, so GTA 6 will fit, but you may need to delete a few big games first. The Xbox Series S has much less space (about 364 GB usable), so Series S owners will almost certainly need to clear room or add storage.

How to make room

  • Delete games you finished. Your saves stay in the cloud or on the console, so you can reinstall later.
  • Move older games to a USB drive. PS5 and Xbox can store last-gen games on cheap external drives.
  • Add an internal SSD. On PS5, an M.2 NVMe SSD with a heatsink works. On Xbox, use an official Seagate or WD expansion card.

Need more space? A 2TB drive gives you room for GTA 6 and the rest of your library.
